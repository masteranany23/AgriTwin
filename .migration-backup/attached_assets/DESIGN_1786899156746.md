## Brand & Style

The design system is rooted in the intersection of **Agricultural Science** and **Advanced Digital Simulation**. It prioritizes clarity, precision, and institutional trust over typical SaaS vibrancy. The brand personality is "The Quiet Authority"—knowledgeable, steady, and technologically sophisticated without being flashy.

The chosen style is **Scientific Minimalism**. It utilizes heavy whitespace to allow complex data to breathe, paired with a restrained color palette and thin, precise borders that echo technical drawings and topographical maps. Visuals should feel "plotted" rather than "painted."

**Key Principles:**
- **Scientific Credibility:** Use precise lines and monospaced accents for data points.
- **Agricultural Context:** Subtle organic textures or color-washes, avoiding literal "dirt and tractors" imagery.
- **Institutional Calm:** Low-energy transitions and a lack of aggressive CTAs to foster a sense of objective research.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by **Deep Forest Green** (#1B3022), providing a sense of depth and environmental stability. This is used primarily for typography, navigation backgrounds, and primary buttons to ensure a high-contrast, premium feel.

**Muted Sage** (#8FA382) acts as the secondary bridging color, used for secondary actions, data series in charts, and decorative elements that require an organic feel without the heaviness of the primary green.

The background is a **Warm Off-White** (#FAF9F6), which prevents the "digital coldness" of pure white and provides a paper-like quality suitable for scientific reports. **Dark Charcoal** (#1F2937) is reserved for body text to ensure maximum legibility. **Earth Amber** is used sparingly as a high-visibility accent for "Observations" or "Critical Metrics."

## Typography

This design system uses **Manrope** for its balance of geometric clarity and humanist warmth. It feels contemporary yet structured, making it ideal for a scientific demonstrator.

- **Headlines:** Use tight letter spacing and semi-bold weights to create a "locked-in" professional look.
- **Labels:** **Inter** is used for small labels and data points to provide a functional, utilitarian contrast to the softer Manrope.
- **Eyebrows:** Use the `label-caps` style for section headers (e.g., "AGRICULTURAL DIGITAL TWIN") to establish a clear hierarchy.
- **Data Display:** For metrics and coordinates, use the medium weight of Inter to ensure individual numbers are distinct and legible at small sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout follows a **Fixed-Width Centered Grid** for desktop to maintain a "report-like" feel, ensuring that data visualizations are never stretched beyond legibility.

- **Grid:** 12-column system with a 24px gutter. 
- **Sectioning:** Large vertical gaps (120px+) between major narrative sections (Hero, Twin, Science) to encourage focused scrolling.
- **Data Cards:** Use a consistent 32px internal padding to maintain the "Generous Whitespace" requirement.
- **Mobile:** Transition to a single-column layout where the Digital Twin visualization is pinned to the top or occupies a 1:1 aspect ratio container to maintain visual impact.

## Elevation & Depth

To avoid the "Generic SaaS" look, this design system eschews heavy drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Layering** and **Subtle Outlines**.

- **Surface Strategy:** Background is `#FAF9F6`. Cards and primary containers use `#FFFFFF`.
- **Borders:** Instead of shadows, use 1px borders in `#E5E7EB` to define boundaries.
- **Active State Elevation:** Only use shadows for interactive elements (hovering over a field marker or a data card). Shadows should be "Long & Ambient"—low opacity (4-6%), high blur (20px+), and slightly tinted with the primary green hue to feel integrated.
- **Data Overlays:** When displaying "Observation" data over the "Simulated" state, use a slight backdrop blur (4px) to suggest a layer of information "floating" over the physical field.

## Shapes

The shape language is **Structured and Soft**. A low roundedness (`0.25rem`) is applied to most UI components to maintain a professional, slightly technical edge. 

- **Data Cards:** Use `rounded-lg` (0.5rem) to differentiate them from the more rigid layout containers.
- **Status Indicators:** Small markers (observations) should be circular to stand out against the linear geometry of crop rows.
- **Buttons:** Standard buttons follow the `0.25rem` rule; only primary "Exploration" CTAs may use a slightly more rounded profile to feel more approachable.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep Forest Green background, White text. No gradients. Solid, authoritative.
- **Secondary:** Transparent background, 1px Deep Forest Green border.
- **Ghost:** Muted Sage text, no border. Used for tertiary navigation.

### Data Cards
White background, 1px subtle border. Title in `label-caps`. Main value in `headline-sm`. Trend indicators (LAI growth) should use Muted Sage for positive or neutral growth, avoiding "Warning Red" unless the data is scientifically critical.

### The Digital Twin Field (2D)
- **Soil:** Warm neutral base.
- **Crops:** Represented by geometric strokes or simple SVG shapes. Density/opacity of the green stroke represents Leaf Area Index (LAI).
- **Observation Markers:** Earth Amber circles with a subtle "pulsing" animation only when a new observation arrives.

### Charts
- **Axes:** Thin `#E5E7EB` lines. No background grid if possible, or very faint dots.
- **Lines:** Primary simulation is a solid Deep Forest Green line. Observation points are Earth Amber dots. Assimilated forecast is a dashed Muted Sage line.

### Input Fields
Clean, 1px borders. Focus state uses a 1px Forest Green border with a 2px soft sage glow. No heavy shadows.
